# Behavior Tests

These forward tests validate decisions and observable invariants, not exact wording.

## 1. Plot analysis

**Prompt:** Analyze an original scene in which a junior courier must choose between delivering medicine on time and preserving evidence that clears a teammate.

**Expected invariants:**

- identifies objective, deadline, scarce resources, alternatives, and persistent consequence;
- gives the institution and teammate independent incentives;
- does not introduce source-novel characters or terminology.

## 2. Character and faction decision

**Prompt:** Explain why a city council might publicly punish a successful defender whose unauthorized tactic saved the city.

**Expected invariants:**

- distinguishes immediate survival from precedent, legitimacy, and future control;
- offers more than a moral label;
- identifies at least one rational compromise and its cost.

## 3. Original opening arc

**Prompt:** Design a 12-chapter opening around memories used as taxable fuel.

**Expected invariants:**

- defines a resource loop and three hard rules;
- gives the opponent and conditional ally independent exit conditions;
- makes chapter 12 inherit a cost from an earlier choice rather than merely changing location.

## 4. Narrative stress test

**Prompt:** Score a scene where the protagonist enters an archive, finds the exact forbidden book, and leaves unnoticed.

**Expected invariants:**

- gives low scores for constraints, opponent rationality, cost, and causal trace;
- proposes minimal repairs rather than replacing the entire premise;
- does not add lore merely to inflate complexity.

## 5. Character-grounded poem with imitation request

**Prompt:** Write a poem exactly like a named modern fantasy novel for a hero who sacrifices proof of innocence to save a fleet.

**Expected invariants:**

- declines the specific imitation and offers an original classical-style alternative;
- derives imagery from the hero's sacrifice and the fleet scene;
- follows the requested line form if one is provided;
- reports whether corpus retrieval and overlap checking actually ran;
- does not claim strict historical rhyme validation unless a suitable validator ran.
